03 / WHERE WE TOW

COLUMBIA HEIGHTS BASED.
MINNESOTA FOCUSED.

Solis Towing is based in Columbia Heights, MN. We generally pick up vehicles within approximately two hours of driving from Columbia Heights, including the Minneapolis–Saint Paul metro and surrounding Minnesota communities. The two-hour guideline applies ONLY to pickups; drop-offs can be anywhere in Minnesota if the trip is approved. This is a driving-time guideline, not a fixed-mile radius or a guarantee of availability.

Common local service areas: Columbia Heights, Minneapolis, Saint Paul, Fridley, New Brighton, Roseville, Blaine, Brooklyn Park, and Bloomington. All pickup and drop-off locations must be in Minnesota. Deliveries beyond the normal pickup area are welcome for consideration, and every route is reviewed before dispatch.
